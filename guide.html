MindAnchor: quick user guide

A calm, private companion for students: focus, rest, stress relief and a path to real help. Everything stays on your own device.

1. Get the app

Demo link: https://mindanchor-mu.vercel.app

  • Android (Chrome): open the link, then tap Install MindAnchor in the Me tab or the browser menu.
  • iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Computer: just open the link. No download needed.

2. Sign in (no real details needed)

  • Continue as Guest to try it straight away.
  • Or Create account with any nickname (not your real name or email) and a password of 6+ characters. Tick the box to confirm your data stays on this device.
  • Passwords can't be reset, because there is no server. Choose one you will remember.
  • Tap Watch demo video for a 20-second tour. It is skippable.

3. The five tabs

TabWhat to do
🏠 TodayTap how you feel (first time, you choose whether check-ins are saved). Pick the Compared myself tag if social media is getting to you, for a short reset. See your next small step and the time you have saved this week.
🎯 Focus & TasksType a big task (for example "Build CS Project") and press Slice to get four 15-minute steps. Press ▶ 15m on a step to start a focus sprint. Tick steps as you finish.
🎧 CalmPlay ambient sounds or a Punjabi rhythm, add your own songs from your device, or paste a Spotify link. Try the breathing exercise or 5-4-3-2-1 grounding. Set a sleep timer.
🛡 GuardLockout: pick night hours when MindAnchor rests and add emergency numbers that always get through. Time caps: tap the apps you want to limit, set minutes, and log your time. A cap can be lowered today but not raised. Pause before you scroll: a 20-second breathing pause, then choose. Phones-down hangout: a 30, 60 or 90-minute timer to put phones away with friends or family.
👤 Me & PrivacyTurn mood saving on or off, download or wipe your data, log out, install the app, and open this guide.

4. Help and safety, always on screen

  • 💙 Get Real Help (bottom right): one tap to helplines for Pakistan, the US or your own saved number.
  • ✕ Quick Exit (ESC) (top right): press it or the ESC key to leave to Google at once.
  • Rest time: if the overnight lockout is on and active, you can still use Calm sounds, Me and Get Real Help.
  • Wipe My Local Data (Me tab) erases everything for this profile.

5. Good to know

MindAnchor is a student prototype, not medical care. A web page cannot see or block other apps on your phone, so caps and lockout apply inside MindAnchor and work as a commitment you log against. If you are struggling, please talk to someone you trust or use Get Real Help.
